Good to know

Are the paid plans available right now?

Not yet. Pro, Business and Elite are shown as upcoming plans. Selecting them here will not charge you or upgrade your account.

Will clicking a plan activate it?

No. This page does not process payments or activate paid subscriptions. Paid upgrades will need a proper payment and account-verification flow before launch.

Why can't I see my current plan?

Sign in to the account connected to your business. If the plan still cannot load, check your connection and try again.
